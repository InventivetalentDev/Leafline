## Pitch

You are a leafcutter ant on an enormous tree. You climb, lay a scent trail and cut a piece out of a leaf. A bigger piece is worth more, but it is slower to carry and catches the wind like a sail. When you send the loaded ant home it walks back along your trail by itself, and you take over the next worker at the nest.

## Director's Cut

- **The cut.** You decide how big each piece is. That size is the trade-off of every trip.
- **The director.** Your scent trail is the direction you give the colony. When a piece is on its way home, recruits walk your route, cut more pieces around your cut and carry them home without you. Where you lay the trail (the sheltered side or the windy side) and how big you cut decide how many of them get home.
- **Your final cut.** The end-of-day recap is titled as the day's cut.
- The theme post says to set the vision, delegate tasks and decide what goes in. Here that is the route you lay, the workers you send along it, and the size of each piece.
- This game is also an idea from the author's notes that had not found a home. It was built with AI agents, which the author directed (see Tools).

## How to play

1. Leave the nest at the base of the trunk. The ant lays a scent trail as it walks.
2. Climb to a leafy branch. Sprint while stamina lasts.
3. At a leaf, hold the cut button. An arc shows the size of the piece, coloured by the wind risk for that size. Release to cut.
4. Press Send home. The loaded ant walks back along its trail and you take over the next worker. Three scouts go out each day.
5. Recruits follow the line, cut pieces from the leaves around your cut and carry them home. Patches run dry, so lay new lines.
6. When a gust warning sounds, hold Grip or get to the side of the tree away from the wind arrow. Hold Tab to see the wind map: dots on the bark around you, green where it is sheltered, red where it is exposed.
7. After the day, read the recap, then spend leaf mass at the nest on Mandibles, Strength, Grip, Stamina and Brood.
8. The colony stays on one tree. Cut leaves do not grow back, so you climb higher and meet stronger wind. The colony moves to a new tree at the second and third milestones. After the third, an end card appears and you can keep playing.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD | Left stick |
| Look | Mouse | Right stick |
| Sprint | Left Shift | Left trigger |
| Cut (hold) | E or left mouse button | X |
| Grip (hold) | Space | A |
| Send home | Q | Y |
| Follow a trail (hold) | F | Right bumper |
| Switch ant (tap) | Tab | Left bumper |
| Wind map (hold): bark dots, green sheltered to red exposed | Tab | Left bumper |
| Pause | Esc or P | Start |

Xbox and PlayStation controllers work. Progress is saved between sessions.

## What is procedural

- The tree, bark, leaves, ground, sky, scent trail and wind effects are generated by code and shaders. The world has no textures and no hand-made tree models; the only images are the button prompt icons in the HUD (CC0, credited below).
- The ant mesh is generated by a Blender Python script. Its walking motion is done in the material.
- Every sound is synthesised by a script.
