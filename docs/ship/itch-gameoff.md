## Pitch

You are a leafcutter ant on an enormous tree. You climb, lay a scent trail and cut a piece out of a leaf. A bigger piece is worth more, but it is slower to carry and catches the wind like a sail. Send the loaded ant home and you shift to the next worker at the nest.

## SHIFT

- **You shift between ants.** Sending a loaded ant home hands you the next worker. The one you sent walks home along its own trail.
- **Each trip is a shift, and a day is a set of shifts.** Three scouts a day. The sun steps from morning to evening with each shift.
- **The ant shifts between surfaces.** It walks on bark in any orientation: up the trunk, along the underside of a branch and out to a twig.
- **The colony shifts trees.** At the second and third milestones it moves to a new, taller tree.

## How to play

1. Leave the nest at the base of the trunk. The ant lays a scent trail as it walks.
2. Climb to a leafy branch. Sprint while stamina lasts.
3. At a leaf, hold the cut button. An arc shows the size of the piece, coloured by the wind risk for that size. Release to cut.
4. Press Send home. The loaded ant walks back along its trail and you take over the next worker.
5. Recruits follow the line your piece makes, cut pieces from the leaves around your cut and carry them home. Patches run dry, so lay new lines.
6. When a gust warning sounds, hold Grip or move to the side of the tree away from the wind arrow. A large piece on an exposed spot is blown off.
7. After the day, read the recap, then spend leaf mass at the nest on Mandibles, Strength, Grip, Stamina and Brood.
8. Cut leaves stay cut and do not grow back, so you climb higher and meet stronger wind. After the third milestone an end card appears and play continues.

## What is procedural

- The tree, bark, leaves, ground, sky, scent trail and wind effects are generated by code and shaders. There are no textures and no hand-made tree models.
- The ant mesh is generated by a Blender Python script, with its walking motion done in the material.
- Every sound is synthesised by a script.

## Tools used

- Unreal Engine 5.8
- Claude (Anthropic): planning and most of the programming, driving the editor through Epic's Model Context Protocol plugin
- Codex (OpenAI): concept art, key art and code reviews
- Blender 5.1 (ant mesh generator script)
- A Python audio synthesis script, checked with the earshot audio analysis tool
