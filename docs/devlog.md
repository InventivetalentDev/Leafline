## How this game is made

Leafline is made by one person directing AI agents. Claude (Anthropic) does planning and most of the programming, driving the Unreal editor through Epic's MCP plugin. Codex (OpenAI) makes concept art and reviews code. The ant model is generated by a Blender script, and the audio is synthesised by a script. The tree, bark, leaves and effects are procedural: no textures and no hand-made tree models.

**Earlier prototype.** On 1–2 November I made a quick prototype of a tree-climbing ant game in a separate project (`TreeAntGame`). No files from it are used here: no code, assets, materials or models. This project was created on 12 November and all game content in it was made from that day on. What did carry over is knowledge: my idea notes (`concept-main.md`), and workflow and engine lessons from that prototype's notes, which the planning agent read (its `CLAUDE.md` and to-do list, not its source code) and used when writing the plan.

## 2026-11-12 (Thu): planning

- Created the project from the Third Person template at 10:05 CET. The template content will be deleted.
- Codex produced five concept images from the idea notes (`Art/Concept/Codex/2026-11-12/initial`). Took four reference photos looking up a real trunk for scale.
- Spent the morning in a planning interview with Claude (Fable 5.2). It read both jams' rules, including the MegaJam Official Rules, and asked me 25 questions over four rounds.
- I had not realised the MegaJam had a theme. "Director's Cut" turned the ant into a **leafcutter**: you choose how big a piece to cut, and your scent trail directs the workers who follow it.
- Game Off's theme, SHIFT, is covered by the same mechanic: sending a loaded ant home shifts you to the next worker. The returning ant simply replays its recorded trail backwards, which made this cheap enough to attempt by Sunday.
- Rocket launchers against birds, from my original notes, are gone. The MegaJam rules exclude anything that reads as animal cruelty, and distraction fits the game better anyway.
- Technical bet: the tree is a distance field. Walking, the camera, wind shelter and trails all query that one function instead of physics collision.
- Wrote `design.md`, `tech.md`, `plan.md`, agent briefs and `CLAUDE.md`. Building moves to a cheaper model from here.
