# Ant mesh

Generated by `Tools/blender/make_ant.py` (Blender 5.1). Run it in Blender to rebuild everything here: `Ant.blend`, `SM_Ant.fbx` and the two previews (`ant_preview_3q.png` three-quarter, `ant_preview_side.png` side).

- One static mesh, one material slot (`M_Ant`, plain brown, placeholder). No rig, no textures. 7,384 triangles, smooth shaded, triangulated.
- Authored in metres: +X forward, +Z up, +Y is the ant's left, origin on the ground under the middle of the thorax, feet at Z = 0.
- Bounds (m): X -0.0967 to 0.1033 (length 0.200), Y -0.0589 to 0.0589, Z 0 to 0.0714.
- FBX is written with Blender's default axes and unit scale; the object should import as 20 cm long. **Check the orientation on import** (nose should point along +X in Unreal). Blender +Y (left) becomes Unreal -Y through the handedness flip, so "left" in the UVs is the ant's left, not +Y in Unreal.
- Legs are modelled in a neutral mid-stride pose; every leg runs hip, knee, ankle, foot, with a ball at each joint.

## Mandible attach point (mesh coordinates, metres)

`(0.0910, 0.0000, 0.0398)`, the point between the two mandible tips, pulled back 4 mm so a carried fragment sits between the jaws. In Unreal (cm, before any import rotation) that is about `X 9.10, Y 0, Z 3.98`.

## UV channels (values as authored in Blender)

| Channel | U | V |
|---|---|---|
| UV0 | Leg group: 0 body/head/antennae/mandibles, 0.25 tripod A (front-left, middle-right, rear-left), 0.75 tripod B (front-right, middle-left, rear-right) | Distance along the leg, hip 0 to foot 1 (0 on the body) |
| UV1 | Part id: 0 body, 0.2 head, 0.4 antennae, 0.6 mandibles, 0.8 legs | Side: 0 left, 1 right (0 on the centre-line body and head) |
| UV2 | Leg pair: 0 front, 0.5 middle, 1 rear | 0 |

Values are per vertex, so they interpolate across triangles only inside one part. Joint balls carry the leg-parameter value of their joint, and the hip (coxa) ball has V = 0. The mandibles and antennae carry UV0 = (0, 0), so they do not move with the gait.

**V is flipped by Unreal on FBX import.** The material sees `1 - V` for every V above: read leg distance as `1 - UV0.v`, side as `1 - UV1.v`. U values are unchanged. Verified in Blender by re-importing the FBX: UV0, UV1 and UV2 all survive with the values above.

# Nest mesh

Generated by `Tools/blender/make_nest.py` (Blender 5.1, fixed seed, so a rerun gives the same mesh). Run it in Blender to rebuild `Nest.blend`, `SM_Nest.fbx` and the two previews: `nest_preview_top.png` (top, orthographic, 3.3 m across) and `nest_preview_3q.png` (three-quarter). The previews show the ant standing on the entrance and a flat ground stand-in for scale. Their soil material is a Blender stand-in that only displays the vertex colours; the real look comes from `Tools/materials/nest_soil.hlsl`.

- One static mesh, two material slots: 0 soil (`M_NestSoil`), 1 leaf scraps (`M_Leaf`). 43,994 triangles, smooth shaded, triangulated, with custom normals. No textures.
- Authored in metres like the ant, so the default FBX export imports at real size in cm. Origin at the mound centre on the ground, +X the actor's forward (toward the trunk), +Z up.
- Bounds in Blender (cm): X -142.3 to 146.9, Y -139.5 to 148.4, Z -0.3 to 7.55. In Unreal, Y is mirrored: Y -148.4 to 139.5.
- **Import:** keep the vertex colours (Replace), and import the normals rather than recomputing them. The custom normals lean inward inside the entrance, so it shades like a hollow, and they smooth the crease of the field's blend near r = 122 cm, which otherwise drew a hard ring of light. No collision is needed; `ANestActor` turns it off.

## The surface is the walking surface

The mound is the walking field's surface, solved per radius from the formulas in `TreeField.cpp` `GroundDistance`: a half ellipsoid of radius 120 cm and height 7 cm, smooth-unioned with the ground at k = 4 cm. Measured at face centres and edge midpoints, the mesh is within 0.25 mm of the field where the field stands higher than 1.5 mm. Further out, from r ≈ 122.9 cm, a skirt rides 1.5 mm above the flat ground disk, so the two do not z-fight. The skirt's outline wanders between 126 and 148 cm, with lobes where spoil was dumped, and its outermost vertices are tucked 3 mm below the ground so the edge is a clean intersection. The match is exact only at 120 / 7: `SetMoundSize` scales the mesh, but the field's 4 cm blend does not scale.

Everything on top is low. The ant is 7 cm tall, with legs about 0.5 cm thick:

- **The lip:** 330 overlapping pellets, 1.2 to 2.4 cm across and up to 6 mm proud. They form a ring from 13 cm out from the centre, outside the ant's feet (about 9 cm) at the start.
- **Spoil:** 70 smaller pellets spread over the mound, more on the camera side.
- **The edge:** 17 piles of overlapping lumps, at the lobes and elsewhere, up to 8 mm above the surface beneath them (9.5 mm above the walking ground on the skirt), with loose crumbs around them and along the rim.
- **Leaf scraps:** 9 scraps, 4.4 to 6.6 cm long, 1.5 mm above the surface, with edges curling up a further 1.5 to 3.2 mm (4.5 mm at most in all).

The entrance is a mean radius of 16 cm with an irregular outline (about 13 to 19 cm), done in vertex colour only. Its geometry stays on the field.

## Vertex colour `Col` (data values, exported LINEAR)

| Channel | Soil (slot 0): mound surface | Soil (slot 0): pellets and clumps | Leaf scraps (slot 1) |
|---|---|---|---|
| R | Soil cover: 1 inside a wandering radius (about 105 to 122 cm, with bays), linear to 0 at the skirt's outline | 1 | 1 (fresh, as on a live leaf) |
| G | Entrance: 1 inside 0.45 × the entrance radius, linear to 0 at 1.05 × | The entrance value at the pellet's centre | Random per scrap (`LeafSet` uses it per leaf) |
| B | 0 | 1 (hard mask) | 0 (height on the tree: ground level) |
| A | Loose spoil near pellets and clumps, 0 to 1 | Random per clump, 0 to 1 | 0 (plain richness) |

Gradients are linear and masks are exactly 0 or 1, so an sRGB conversion on import would change only the shape of the ramps. After import, check one mid value. For example, G halfway out of the entrance should read about 0.5 in the material's VertexColor, not about 0.73.

## UV0

- Soil: planar position, `(x / 240 + 0.5, y / 240 + 0.5)` in cm. It runs a little past 0 to 1 on the skirt.
- Leaf scraps: each scrap's bounding box, 0 to 1. U runs along the scrap's long axis and V across it. The straight leaf-margin side makes the shape asymmetric, so V = 0.5 runs along the middle of the box. Unreal flips V on import, which is harmless here.
